How Multi-AZ keeps orders flowing

design

Every tier exists in us-east-1a and us-east-1b. If one zone fails, the load balancer stops sending traffic there, the RDS standby is promoted automatically (≈1–2 min, no data loss) and both Auto Scaling groups launch replacements in the healthy zone.

Orders already in SQS wait safely; SWF re-schedules any step whose worker disappeared. Press Simulate AZ failure above to see the paths that keep working.
